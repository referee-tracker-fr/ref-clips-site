How it works

Three steps. That’s it.

Keep your habits: the game video and your referee’s eye. REF CLIPS does the cutting and the filing.

1

Pick your game

Sign in with your Referee Tracker account: your games are already there. Add the video, a file or a Drive, Dropbox or WeTransfer link.

2

Tag your calls

One key per play while it runs. Change a call, add a note, draw on the frame. Everything saves on its own.

3

Export

One click: clips cut to the second, sorted by theme, with a PDF report ready for your referee coach.

    Features

    Everything you need to break down a game.

    Keyboard tagging

    Type the start of a call or click a theme. The clip is created around the play, in the right quarter.

    Favourites and BEST

    Your 9 favourite calls on keys 1 to 9. Your finest moments as BEST, gathered apart.

    Draw on the frame

    Arrows, circles, lines and text in bright colours, frozen for 3 seconds in the exported clip.

    Exact clips, up to 4K

    Cut to the second, in original quality or a light version for sharing.

    PDF report

    With every export: calls by theme and by quarter, BEST, notes. Ready for your coach.

    Two-screen mode

    Full-screen video on one side, the calls on the other. With one screen, a floating window.

    Video from a link

    Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, WeTransfer or a direct link: paste it, REF CLIPS downloads it.

    Referee Tracker account

    Your name, level and games are already there. No form to fill in.

    Questions

    What referees ask us.

    What software can basketball referees use for video analysis?

    REF CLIPS, built by a referee: tag your calls on the keyboard while the video plays, the software cuts each play into a clip, sorts it by theme and prepares a PDF report for your coach. On Mac and Windows, with your Referee Tracker account.

    Are my videos uploaded?

    No. Your videos, clips, notes and reports stay on your computer. If you agree, REF CLIPS only sends usage statistics to improve. Details in the privacy policy (in French).

    Which videos work?

    MP4, MOV, MTS, AVI and other camera and phone formats, up to 4K, in one or several files. Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, WeTransfer and direct links too. YouTube does not allow downloads: ask the club for the file.

    Do I need internet?

    Only to sign in and to fetch a video from a link. Analysis and export work offline.

    We are a 3-person crew.

    Choose “3 referees” in the settings: the Centre calls and the triple whistle appear. You can also mark which referee each clip is about.

    A problem or an idea?

    In REF CLIPS, open ⋯ › Report a problem: your message goes straight to the team.
